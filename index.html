How a launch works

Three steps. The payouts run on their own after that.

01

Make your coin

Name, ticker, image. Pair it with SOL or a tokenized stock. Every coin gets its own escrow vault.

02

Set the lock schedule

Max 5x, 7.5x or 10x, over short, standard or long schedules. Lockers who commit longer earn more weight.

03

Lockers get paid

60% of the fees go to your coin’s lockers every 10 minutes in SOL. When a lock ends, unlock or renew to keep earning.

Five ways to release

Pick the agreement that fits the deal. Conditions are fixed when the escrow is created.

Time-locked

Funds unlock on a set date.

Milestone

Funds release when a named verifier confirms the milestone.

Mutual

Both parties approve before anything moves.

Streaming

Funds unlock a little at a time between two dates.

Reward

Tokens stay locked and earn SOL from trading fees, up to 7.5x.

Create an agreement

Set the asset, the recipient and the release rule. Everything is visible on-chain once it’s created.

Summary
Milestones, disputes and cancellation rules must be agreed up front. Not every off-chain event can be verified automatically, so milestone escrows rely on the verifier you name.
